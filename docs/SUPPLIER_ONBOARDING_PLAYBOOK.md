# FactoryScout — Supplier Onboarding Playbook

**Purpose:** Convert public-source factory prospects into permissioned, reviewable supplier profiles before buyer acquisition.

**Current pipeline:** Five researched Tunisian apparel prospects are in the admin-only pipeline. All are at `researched`; none has been contacted, approved, or independently verified.

## 1. Outreach sequence

1. Use the official business contact channel shown on the prospect's own website.
2. Introduce FactoryScout as an early-stage international sourcing marketplace focused on making manufacturer capabilities and evidence easier for buyers to compare.
3. Ask whether the company is open to a no-cost initial profile and a short verification call.
4. Do not promise buyer volume, orders, exclusivity, ranking, or guaranteed leads.
5. Do not publish logos, photos, detailed capacity figures, certification badges or contact details without explicit permission.
6. Record the date, channel, person, reply and next action in the admin pipeline. Change status only when the event actually occurs.

## 2. First-contact email template

**Subject:** Invitation to review your factory profile on FactoryScout

Hello,

I'm reaching out from FactoryScout, an early-stage B2B sourcing marketplace helping international apparel brands discover and compare manufacturing partners in Tunisia and nearby markets.

We are preparing our initial manufacturer directory and came across your company through its public website. We would like to ask whether you would be interested in reviewing a basic company profile on FactoryScout.

There is no charge to review the initial profile. Before publishing anything, we would confirm the information with your team and obtain permission for any company description, contact details, images or documents shown on the platform.

To assess whether the profile is useful to buyers, we would appreciate confirmation of:
- Your main garment categories and production processes
- Which operations are performed in-house versus subcontracted
- Typical minimum order quantities and sample process
- Indicative lead times and current available capacity
- Export markets and current certification documents, if applicable
- The best person to review and approve the company profile

FactoryScout does not claim to certify factories or guarantee orders. Supplier-provided information is labelled as such, and any independent checks are recorded separately.

Would you be open to a short introductory conversation?

Best regards,  
FactoryScout Supplier Partnerships

## 3. Verification checklist

Before a supplier account is approved, an admin reviewer should confirm:

- Legal entity name and registration evidence.
- Authority of the applicant to represent the business.
- Operating factory address and ownership/lease/affiliation relationship.
- Product categories, machinery and production processes.
- In-house versus subcontracted operations.
- MOQ, sample process, lead times and current capacity.
- Export experience and documentation.
- Certificates: holder, issuing body, scope, site covered and expiry date.
- Permission to publish the company profile, contact details and supplied media.
- A working supplier account and successful test RFQ/quotation exchange.

## 4. Claiming an existing FactoryScout profile

If the company already appears in the directory:
- Ask the applicant to sign up as a supplier and select the existing profile.
- Match the applicant to the legal entity and verify their authority.
- Review the website, company email domain and supporting documents.
- Approve only after ownership is sufficiently established.
- Approval links the account to the existing profile; it does not automatically verify production claims or certifications.

## 5. Follow-up policy

- First follow-up: 4–5 business days after the initial message.
- Final follow-up: 7–10 business days later.
- If there is no response, mark the lead as unresponsive/not a fit as appropriate and stop repeated contact.
- If the company declines, record the reason and respect the request.
- Do not send messages from a personal account or represent FactoryScout as an established large marketplace.

## 6. Go-live rule

A supplier is not RFQ-capable merely because it has been researched or contacted. It must complete supplier signup, pass ownership review, receive admin approval and have a working account capable of receiving and answering test RFQs. Publicly visible profile claims remain supplier-stated until independently checked.
