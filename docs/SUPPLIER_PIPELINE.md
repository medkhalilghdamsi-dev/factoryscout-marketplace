## Standard verification checklist before approval

For every prospect, collect and review:

1. Legal company name, registration details and authorized representative.
2. Proof that the applicant is authorized to represent the factory or company.
3. Factory address and whether production is owned, leased, affiliated or subcontracted.
4. Product categories and processes actually performed in-house.
5. MOQ by product/style, sample process and charges, lead times and current available capacity.
6. Export markets and ability to provide export/customs documentation.
7. Current certificates with holder name, scope, issuing body and expiry date.
8. Permission to publish the company profile, images, capabilities and contact details on FactoryScout.
9. Supplier account approval and test RFQ delivery to the actual supplier account.
